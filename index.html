How to play
  • ← / → or A / D — dodge left and right. On phone, swipe left/right or hold Juke.
  • ↑ / W — sprint. You cover yards faster, but defenders close faster too.
  • ↓ / S — slow down. Useful in traffic to let a hole open up.
  • Tackled? You lose a down and get pushed back 10 yards. Four tackles ends the game.
  • Touchdown? +1 score, downs reset, and the next drive is faster with more defenders.
